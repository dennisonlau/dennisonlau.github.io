## Why I write

Finishing a book is one thing. Explaining what I think about it takes more work.

I write to work out what stayed with me, where I disagree, and what might change how I approach a problem. Putting those thoughts into words helps me notice gaps in my understanding and express myself more clearly.

My [Bookshelf](/bookshelf/) collects those reading notes. [Essays](/essays/) is where I explore other questions along the way.

These are thoughts in progress. If something resonates with you—or you see it differently—I'd enjoy hearing your perspective.
