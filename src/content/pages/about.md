I design low-power ASICs. My work involves thinking about how
different parts of a system fit together, and the trade-offs
that shape the whole.

Away from work, I read about technology, investing, and how
people think. This site is where I take those ideas a little
further.

## Why I write

Finishing a book is one thing. Explaining what I think about
it takes more work.

I write to work out what stayed with me, where I disagree,
and what might change how I approach a problem. Putting those
thoughts into words helps me notice gaps in my understanding
and express myself more clearly.

My [Bookshelf](/bookshelf/) collects those reading notes.
[Essays](/essays/) is where I explore other questions along
the way.

These are thoughts in progress. If something resonates with
you—or you see it differently—I'd enjoy hearing your perspective.
